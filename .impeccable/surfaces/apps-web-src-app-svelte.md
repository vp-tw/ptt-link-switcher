# PTT Link Switcher web surface

- Scope and mode: `apps/web/src/App.svelte`; Operate with a marketing-grade first viewport.
- Audience and job: Traditional Chinese PTT readers paste a supported URL, share text, or full AID and immediately choose a preferred reader.
- Primary action: paste or enter input, inspect the canonical board/article reference, then copy or open one reader URL.
- Proof: the conversion mechanism and four generated provider links. No metadata, testimonials, usage metrics, or network-derived proof.
- Constraints: pure frontend; conversion remains offline; fixed theme; GitHub footer link; modern evergreen browsers; complete keyboard use and reduced motion.
- Chosen direction: Taiwan Routing Label. Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/model-pick.webp`.
- Desktop composition: Horizontal Dispatch Desk. A full-width input manifest resolves into a canonical routing strip above four equal provider tickets.
- Mobile adaptation: input and canonical strip remain full width; provider tickets become a complete vertical list ordered by preference, with the default reader first. Do not hide choices in a carousel.
- Memorable moment: successful parsing visibly stamps the canonical route and dispatches the provider tickets into place.

## Component grammar

- Corners: clipped or perforated ticket edges; avoid generic rounded rectangles.
- Lines: 2px navy routing rules, heavier dividers at dispatch boundaries.
- Elevation: physical overlap through offset paper shadows; no glass or floating SaaS cards.
- Type ramp: oversized condensed product/display text, strong operational labels, mono URL/reference data, compact utility captions.
- Density: dense and legible on desktop; vertical, touch-safe, and fully enumerated on mobile.
